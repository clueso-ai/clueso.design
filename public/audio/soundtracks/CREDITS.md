# Soundtrack credits

Every track here is released under the Creative Commons CC0 1.0 Universal public domain dedication
(https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). No attribution is required and commercial use
and redistribution are allowed. We credit the artists anyway.

Each track page on the Free Music Archive states the licence as "CC0 1.0 Universal License" and links
to the CC0 deed. Licence checked on 2026-11-05.

All five files were edited the same way:

- Trimmed to a 55 s section of the original, skipping the intro (start time listed per track)
- 2 s fade in and 3 s fade out
- Loudness normalised to about -16 LUFS integrated, true peak at or below -1.5 dBTP (ffmpeg loudnorm, two pass, linear)
- Stereo, 44.1 kHz, AAC at about 128 kb/s in .m4a
- Embedded cover art and tags removed
