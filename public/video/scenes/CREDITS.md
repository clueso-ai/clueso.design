# Scene credits

The two default scenes in the Transition lab are public domain works of the US federal government,
taken from Wikimedia Commons. No attribution is required and commercial use and redistribution are
allowed. We credit the authors anyway.

Each Commons file page carries a public domain licence template, linked per clip below. Licence
checked on 2026-11-05. NASA asks that its footage is not used to imply endorsement, and that its
insignia is not used. Neither clip shows a logo, text or a person.

Both files were edited the same way:

- Cut to a 5 s section of the original (start time listed per clip)
- Scaled from 1920×1080 to 1280×720, 30 fps
- H.264 High profile, yuv420p, faststart, no audio track
- A poster JPG taken from 2.5 s into the cut
